06Performance marketing

Paid media with a reason to exist.

Boosting a post is not a strategy. I build campaigns around a defined objective, a specific audience and creative that earns the click — then optimise against the number that matters.

Every campaign starts with one question: what would make this spend worth it?

The answer becomes the structure. Audience segments, creative tests, landing path and tracking all point at the same outcome. When the data disagrees with the plan, the plan changes.

07SEO & organic growth

Found before they search again.

Search is where intent becomes visible. I work on the foundations, the local listing and the content that answers the query — then report what actually changed.

For local businesses, the first impression is often a Google Business Profile. For content-led brands, it is a search result. Both need the same discipline: accurate information, useful pages and a reason to choose you before the click.

13Working process

Seven steps, no mystery.

Every engagement starts with a written scope: objectives, deliverables, timelines, reporting and what sits outside the agreed work.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Business, offer, audience and the commercial goal.

  2. 02

    Research

    Market, category, search demand and what competitors are actually doing.

  3. 03

    Strategise

    A written plan: channels, content, campaigns, scope and measurement.

  4. 04

    Create

    Creative, copy and assets built for the platform they live on.

  5. 05

    Execute

    Publishing, campaign build and community management within scope.

  6. 06

    Measure

    Monthly reporting against the objective, with the source stated.

  7. 07

    Optimise

    Change what the data supports; leave what already works alone.
